Personal health record

JW HomeChart

One family's medical chart, kept at home on its own computer. It isn't offered to the public, and there's nothing to sign up for.

What it does

It keeps a family's lab results, visits, medications, imaging and notes in one place, so they're easy to look back on and to share with a doctor.

With each person's permission, it reads their records from athenahealth, Epic (MyChart) and Oura through each service's own patient sign-in. Everyone signs in with their own account and chooses what to share.

Privacy

  • Kept at homeRecords are stored only on the family's own computer. There's no cloud service and no copy on anyone else's servers.
  • Read onlyIt reads records. It never changes anything in a medical record.
  • Never sharedNothing is sent to anyone outside the family. There are no analytics, ads or trackers, and no data is ever sold or shared.
  • Always your choiceAccess can be withdrawn at any time, in the app or from the patient portal. Disconnecting deletes the app's access.
